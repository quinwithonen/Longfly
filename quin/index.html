FPV Micro Gimbal

A two-axis head-tracking gimbal for the DJI O4 Lite camera, small enough for a micro quad. Two 1.5 g linear servos push printed racks through 9 mm of stroke, and the gearing turns that into 281° of pan.

GearingModule 0.5, 25° PA · pan 1.5:1 step-up
Footprint26.5 × 27.1 mm, 54.2 mm tall
Mount4× M2 on 20 × 20 mm
Rev B changePan range ±93.8° → ±140.6°

Checked in CAD at 15 pan/tilt combinations, every end stop included, with no clashes. Flight testing is next.

Magnetic levitation rig

A one-axis levitator I designed, assembled and programmed from scratch. Two Hall-effect sensors read where the floating rod is, and an Arduino running my control code keeps adjusting the electromagnet to hold it in the air.

ActuatorElectromagnet
Sensing2× Hall-effect sensors
