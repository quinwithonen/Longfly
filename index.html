About Longfly

About Longfly

LONGFLY develops realistic cockpit systems that bring the pilot's perspective to RC aviation.

Using head-tracking technology and live FPV video, our cockpits create an immersive flying experience. Our current F-16 design features functional flight instruments, moving controls, and compatibility with the Motion RC V3 70mm F-16 and other RC aircraft with wingspans over three feet.

Our mission is simple: Bring the pilot's perspective to RC aviation.
