The story

Built at a desk in Boston,
at 3 a.m., by one student.

I'm Quinlong — lifelong FPV hobbyist, and a dude who's crazy about one day flying a real fighter jet. Every cockpit is designed, printed, wired and tested by me at my desk in Boston. Thanks for supporting my project.

Handmade, every one

Each and every unit is handmade by me, with hours of work in every single one. Backing early does not mean sub-par quality — you get excellent, handmade quality. It's just a huge pain in my ass to make, because it's all by hand.

What founders fund

Your deposit pays for the custom circuit board that replaces the hand-wiring — the step from ten handmade units to a real product. You're not buying a toy; you're starting a company with me. And honestly? You're gonna save my back and help me reclaim my social life — the boards mean I don't have to spend so many hours wiring and soldering in the future. For that, I'm deeply grateful.

FAQ

Questions, answered straight

Is the deposit really refundable?
Yes. Any time until your unit ships — one email to me, refund goes back to your card. No forms, no phone call, no hard feelings.

What if it doesn't ship in time for Christmas?
Your choice: full deposit back, or keep your founders price and be first in Batch 2. I won't hold your money hostage to my schedule.

What exactly do I get?
The cockpit unit for your F-16 RC jet — displays, HUD, backlit panels, controls — ready to install. The jet and FPV goggles aren't included; the cockpit works with the DJI FPV setup.

Why is Batch 1 half price?
As a reward for being so generous as to give my project a chance to live. That's what the Supporters discount is: $200 instead of $400, handmade, numbered. When these ten are gone, they're gone.

Waitlist vs deposit — what's the difference?
The waitlist is a queue for news and Batch 2. The deposit is a held unit, a locked $200 price, and a Christmas ship date. Only the deposit holds a cockpit.
