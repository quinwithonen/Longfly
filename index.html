The story

Built at a desk in Boston,
at 3 a.m., by one student.

I'm Quinlong — a mechanical engineering student. I saw a cockpit like this flying in China, watched the clip pull 300,000 views in five days, and realised nobody sells it in the West. So I started building it: designing, printing, soldering flex cables finer than a fingernail, losing fights with CAD at 2 a.m.

This is V1

Handmade. Not perfect. The first one that exists — and it only gets better from here. I'm not going to pretend it's a finished factory product; the videos show exactly what it is.

What founders fund

Your deposit pays for the custom circuit board that replaces the hand-wiring — the step from ten handmade units to a real product. You're not buying a toy; you're starting a company with me.

Questions, answered straight

Is the deposit really refundable?
Yes. Any time until your unit ships — one email to me, refund goes back to your card. No forms, no phone call, no hard feelings.
What if it doesn't ship in time for Christmas?
Your choice: full deposit back, or keep your founders price and be first in Batch 2. I won't hold your money hostage to my schedule.
What exactly do I get?
The cockpit unit for your F-16 RC jet — displays, HUD, backlit panels, controls — ready to install. The jet and FPV goggles aren't included; the cockpit works with the DJI FPV setup.
Why is Batch 1 half price?
Because you're paying before it exists. That's faith, and faith gets a discount: $200 instead of $400, handmade, numbered. When these ten are gone, they're gone.
Waitlist vs deposit — what's the difference?
The waitlist is a queue for news and Batch 2. The deposit is a held unit, a locked $200 price, and a Christmas ship date. Only the deposit holds a cockpit.
